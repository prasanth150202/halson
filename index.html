Armani Hallson KLCC sits directly on Jalan Ampang, within walking distance of Suria KLCC, KLCC Park, and the Petronas Twin Towers themselves. It is a short list of buildings in this city that can claim an unobstructed line of sight to the skyline — this is one of them.

Every residence is designed around that view: floor-to-ceiling glazing, deep balconies, and a material palette of stone, timber and brushed bronze that reads as quiet rather than loud.
